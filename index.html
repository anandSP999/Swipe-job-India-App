About this app

Find your next job — one swipe at a time 🇮🇳

Swipe Job India is a fast, secure and modern recruitment platform built for India's job market. Whether you are a fresher looking for your first opportunity or an experienced professional planning a switch, the app connects you directly with verified employers near you.

✨ Key Features

  • Swipe to apply — discover relevant jobs with a simple swipe.
  • Smart matching — jobs recommended by your skills, location & experience.
  • One-tap apply — apply instantly using your saved profile.
  • Verified employers — every company is screened before posting.
  • Real-time alerts — get notified the moment a matching job is posted.
  • Dedicated client portal — employers can post jobs and hire talent.
  • Secure & private — your data stays protected.
  • Lightweight — works smoothly even on low-end devices and slow networks.

👤 For Candidates

Create your profile in under 2 minutes, upload your resume, and start swiping. Track every application in one place, save jobs for later, and chat directly with recruiters.

🏢 For Employers / Clients

Post a job in seconds, browse verified candidate profiles, and shortlist the right talent without paying hefty consultancy fees.

🔒 Safe & Trusted

Swipe Job India does not ask for any payment for applying to jobs. Beware of fraudsters asking for money in the name of job offers.
